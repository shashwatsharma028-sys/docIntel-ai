Northwind Logistics Travel and Expense Policy (2024 edition, effective 1 April 2024)

Scope. This policy applies to all Northwind employees and contractors travelling on company business from 1 April 2024. It replaces the 2022 travel policy in full. Exceptions require written approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

Air travel. Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires CFO approval and is limited to flights over ten hours.

Hotels. The nightly room limit is 180 euros in standard cities and 260 euros in designated high-cost cities, which are London, Zurich, New York and Tokyo. Rates above the limit need line manager approval before booking.

Meals. Employees may claim a daily meal allowance of 65 euros on travel days. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment is claimed separately under the hospitality procedure with the names of all attendees.

Mileage. Use of a private car is reimbursed at 0.42 euros per kilometre up to 400 kilometres per trip. Longer journeys must use rail unless the line manager approves the car in advance.

Claims. Expense claims must be submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of returning, with itemised receipts for every item above 25 euros. Claims submitted after 90 days are rejected.
