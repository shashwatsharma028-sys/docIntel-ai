Northwind Logistics Travel Policy (2022 edition, superseded)

Scope. This 2022 policy applies to employees travelling on company business. Contractors are covered by their own agreements. Exceptions require approval from the Finance Director.

Air travel. Economy class is required for flights under eight hours. Business class may be booked for flights of eight hours or longer with Finance Director approval.

Hotels. The nightly room limit is 150 euros in all cities. Employees should prefer hotels on the corporate rate list.

Meals. The daily meal allowance is 50 euros. Receipts are required for meals above 15 euros.

Claims. Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the end of the trip. Late claims may be paid at the discretion of the Finance Director.
