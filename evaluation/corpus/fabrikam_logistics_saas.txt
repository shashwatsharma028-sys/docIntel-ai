Fabrikam Route Optimisation SaaS Subscription Terms

Term and renewal. The subscription runs for 12 months and renews automatically for further 12-month terms unless the customer cancels at least 30 days before renewal through the account portal.

Fees and price changes. Fabrikam may increase subscription fees at renewal by up to 7 percent per year. Price increases above 7 percent allow the customer to terminate without penalty within 30 days of notice.

Service levels. Fabrikam targets 99.5 percent monthly availability. Service credits of 5 percent of the monthly fee apply below the target and must be requested within 15 days of the end of the month.

Liability. Fabrikam's liability is capped at the fees paid in the 3 months before the claim, and Fabrikam is not liable for indirect or consequential losses, including lost profits.

Data. Customer route data may be used by Fabrikam in aggregated and anonymised form to improve its routing models. Fabrikam will notify customers of a data breach within 72 hours.
